The company is engaged in providing software solution and wide range of IT Services.

Infosys Limited, also popularly known as Infosys, is a listed public company incorporated on 02 July, 1981. It is classified as a public limited company and is located in Karnataka, Karnataka. It's authorized share capital is INR 2,400.00 cr and the total paid-up capital is INR 2,029.12 cr.


Infosys Limited's operating revenue range is Over INR 500 cr for the financial year ending on 31 March, 2026. It's EBITDA has increased by 7.5% over the previous year. At the same time, it's book networth has decreased by 7.39%.

The current status of Infosys Limited is - Active.

The last reported AGM (Annual General Meeting) of Infosys Limited, per our records, was held on 23 June, 2026.

The company has 12 directors - Salil Satish Parekh, Bobby Kanubhai Parikh, and others.

The Corporate Identification Number (CIN) of Infosys Limited is L85110KA1981PLC013115. It's registered address is at ELECTRONICS CITY,HOSUR ROAD, BANGALORE, KARNATAKA, Karnataka - 560100.
