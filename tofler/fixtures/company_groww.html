GROWW INVEST TECH PRIVATE LIMITED
Active
Updated: 16 Oct 2026

GROWW INVEST TECH PRIVATE LIMITED is a financial technology company operating in the investment and wealth management sector. It provides a suite of services including robo-advisory, mutual fund investments, and portfolio management solutions, empowering individuals and businesses to make informed financial decisions and optimize their investment strategies.

Groww Invest Tech Private Limited, is an unlisted private company incorporated on 04 May, 2016. It is classified as a private limited company and is located in Bangalore South, Karnataka. It's authorized share capital is INR 20.00 cr and the total paid-up capital is INR 6.63 cr.


Groww Invest Tech's operating revenue range is Over INR 500 cr for the financial year ending on 31 March, 2025. It's EBITDA has increased by 45.6% over the previous year. At the same time, it's book networth has increased by 47.56%.

The current status of Groww Invest Tech Private Limited is - Active.

The last reported AGM (Annual General Meeting) of Groww Invest Tech Private Limited, per our records, was held on 27 July, 2026.

The company has 9 directors - Harsh Jain, Ishan Bansal, and others.

The Corporate Identification Number (CIN) of Groww Invest Tech Private Limited is U65100KA2016PTC092879. It's registered address is at Vaishnavi Tech Park, South Tower, 3rd Floor, Survey No.16/1 and 17/2, Ambalipura Village, Varthur Hobli, Bellandur, Bangalore South, Karnataka - 560103.
